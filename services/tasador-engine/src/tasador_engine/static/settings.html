Stage 1: Ingestion & Normalization images.py · pypdfium2

Decodes incoming files (JPEG, PNG, HEIC from iPhones, multi-page PDF invoices). Normalizes EXIF orientation, strips GPS coordinates & device metadata for privacy, and downscales to max 1568 px.

↓
🏷️
Stage 2: Local Barcode Scanner (ZXing) zxingcpp (C++) · ~15ms

Direct pixel scanner checks for EAN-13, UPC-A, Code-128, and QR codes across all photos and PDF pages. Detects manufacturer GTIN barcodes with mathematical checksum verification.

↓
📝
Stage 3: Local Printed Text OCR (RapidOCR) ONNX Runtime · ~350ms

Runs deep learning OCR model locally to extract text lines from equipment chassis, serial plates, invoice line items, and VAT totals. Groups text per photo and merges shared vocabulary.

↓
🧠
Stage 4: Multimodal Vision AI Ollama / Claude / OpenAI

Multimodal LLM synthesizes visual geometry with OCR nameplate text and barcodes. Identifies exact Brand, Model, Equipment Type, Serial Number, Confidence %, and generates targeted search queries.

↓
🌐
Stage 5: Web Search & Market Pricing SearXNG (Docker) / Brave

Runs structured queries (GTIN query, Brand + Model query). Fetches top candidate shop pages, extracts schema.org Product metadata, verifies GTIN agreement, drops statistical outliers, and calculates New & Used price ranges in target currency.

↓
📦
Stage 6: Consolidated Synthesis pipeline.py

Consolidates final entity schema with evidence tags (e.g. barcode:1, ocr:printed-text, gtin:from-shop-page), duplicates warning, latency waterfall, and verified shop offer URLs.
